**World bosses** spawn on a fixed schedule and often need guild coordination — but can be soloed with enough GS and the right build.

## Active bosses

### Count Drakenmoor
- **Zone:** Count's Fortress
- **Spawn:** Every Saturday 20:00 server time
- **Mechanic:** Fire spirit phase — switch to frost weapons
- **Loot:** Epic+ weapons, **Drakenmoor Essence**

### Murkcrown
- **Zone:** Blood Marsh (deepest area)
- **Spawn:** Every 48 hours
- **Mechanic:** Stacking poison — bring an antidote trinket
- **Loot:** Set pieces for **Murkcrown Armor**

## Reward table (simplified)

| Placement | Reward |
|-----------|--------|
| Top 10% DPS | Legendary chest |
| Participation | Epic chest |
| Guild bonus | +15% drop if 5+ members participate |

## Preparation

1. Max out flasks before spawn
2. Set loot filter to Epic+
3. Enable **Boss Mode** in combat settings (more DPS, less sustain)
