Idle Hunter blends classic **idle progression** with deep **MMORPG-style** customization. Your hunter keeps fighting, looting, and leveling even when you are away.

## Your first hour

1. **Pick a starter class** — each class has a unique playstyle (see [Hunter Classes](/wiki/hunter-classes)).
2. **Enable auto-hunt** — let your character passively farm the first zone.
3. **Upgrade weapons early** — DPS matters more than defense in Act 1.
4. **Complete tutorial quests** — they unlock guild features and offline rewards.

## Idle mechanics

| Mechanic | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Offline gains | Up to 12 hours of accumulated XP and gold |
| Prestige | Reset level for permanent bonuses after Act 3 |
| Auto-loot | Filter by rarity in settings |
| Party sync | Share passive bonuses with guild members |

> **Tip:** Log in at least once per day to claim daily rewards and refresh your offline cap.

## Next steps

At **level 25**, **Shadow Vale** opens — your first real grind zone. Read more in [World & Zones](/wiki/world-zones).
