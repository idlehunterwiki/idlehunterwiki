Idle Hunter has **six main classes**, each with two specializations at level 40.

## Class overview

| Class | Role | Strength |
|-------|------|----------|
| Ranger | Ranged DPS | Crit damage, poison |
| Knight | Tank | Block, aggro |
| Arcanist | Magic DPS | AoE, mana burn |
| Shadow Walker | Assassin | Burst, dodge |
| Priest | Support/Heal | Buffs, resurrection |
| Berserker | Melee DPS | Rage, lifesteal |

## Recommended for beginners

**Ranger** and **Knight** are the most forgiving:

- Ranger needs minimal micro-management in idle mode.
- Knight survives longer boss fights without manual healing.

## Specializations (level 40)

### Ranger
- **Pathfinder** — single-target and boss DPS
- **Venom Weaver** — DoT and crowd control

### Knight
- **Temple Guardian** — pure tank with party shields
- **Warlord** — hybrid DPS/tank

Build your first talent tree using the [Skill Tree](/wiki/skill-tree) guide.
