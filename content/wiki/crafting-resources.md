## Currencies

| Currency | Use |
|----------|-----|
| Gold | Forging, trading, respec |
| Gems | Premium shop, inventory expansion |
| Guild coins | Guild shop, buffs |
| Prestige fragments | Permanent upgrades |

## Common materials

- **Iron Ore** — Act 1, Stone Quarry
- **Dark Essence** — Act 2, Blood Marsh
- **Ember Crystal** — Act 3, smeltery dungeon
- **Drakenmoor Essence** — world boss drop

## Crafting stations

Unlocked in **Ruin City** (level 20):
- **Forge** — weapons and armor
- **Alchemist** — flasks and buffs
- **Enchanter** — sockets and infusion

## Tips for idle players

Enable **auto-craft** when materials hit cap. Prioritize flasks over gear if you are pushing bosses.
