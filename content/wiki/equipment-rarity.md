All equipment in Idle Hunter has a **rarity** that determines stat count and max values.

## Rarity tiers

| Color | Name | Stats |
|-------|------|-------|
| Gray | Worn | 1 |
| Green | Fine | 2 |
| Blue | Rare | 3 |
| Purple | Epic | 4 + possible set bonus |
| Orange | Legendary | 5 + unique effect |
| Red | Mythic | 6 + socket |

## Gear Score

Your **Gear Score (GS)** affects:
- Access to higher zones
- Guild ranking
- Damage scaling against world bosses

**Formula (simplified):** `GS = Σ (item level × rarity multiplier)`

## Upgrading

- **Forge** — spend iron and gold for +1 to +10
- **Infusion** — add secondary stats (level 50+)
- **Transmog** — cosmetic only, no stat impact

See [Crafting & Resources](/wiki/crafting-resources) for material sources.
