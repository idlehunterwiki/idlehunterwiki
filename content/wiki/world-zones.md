The world of Idle Hunter is split into **acts**, each with several zones.

## Act 1 — Starter Realm (level 1–30)

| Zone | Level | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Grindwood | 1–10 | Tutorial, basic loot |
| Stone Quarry | 10–18 | First elite mobs |
| Ruin City | 18–30 | First dungeon: **The Crypt** |

## Act 2 — Shadow Vale (level 30–55)

- **Mist Heights** — poison resist recommended
- **Blood Marsh** — crafting material: **Dark Essence**
- **Count's Fortress** — weekly world boss spawn

## Act 3 — Ember Frontier (level 55–80)

Endgame starts here. You need **GS 450+** to survive.

## Dungeons vs open world

- **Open world:** best for idle gold/hour
- **Dungeons:** best for gear and set pieces (requires keys)

Boss guide: [World Bosses](/wiki/world-bosses).
