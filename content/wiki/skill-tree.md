Each level grants **1 talent point**. Max level 100 gives 100 points — but the tree has over 200 nodes, so you must prioritize.

## Branches

Each class has three branches:
1. **Offensive** — damage and crit
2. **Defensive** — survival and sustain
3. **Utility** — idle bonuses, loot, speed

## Key nodes

- **Keystone (level 30)** — defines your build direction
- **Capstone (level 70)** — powerful passive, one per branch
- **Cross-spec (level 50)** — requires 20 points in another branch

## Respec

- First respec: **free**
- After that: 500 gold + 1 **Scroll of Forgetting** (dungeon drop)
- **Prestige** resets all points at no cost

## Idle synergies

For passive farming, prioritize nodes that increase:
- **Attack speed** (more hits = more procs)
- **Area damage** (more mobs per tick)
- **Offline efficiency** (Utility branch)
