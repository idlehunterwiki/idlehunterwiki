When you reach **level 80** and defeat the Act 3 boss **Ember Lord**, **Prestige** unlocks.

## What is Prestige?

Prestige **resets** your character level to 1 but keeps:
- Equipment (optional wipe for bonus)
- Guild membership
- **Prestige fragments** (permanent stat boosts)

## Prestige fragments

Each prestige grants fragments based on:
- Highest level before reset
- Epic+ items kept
- Bosses defeated during the run

**Recommended first prestige:** GS 500+, level 80.

## Paragon (after prestige 3)

Paragon levels grant **0.1%** bonus per level to all stats — no cap, but diminishing returns after level 200.

## Endgame loop

1. Push new zone → farm gear
2. Optimize build → world boss
3. Prestige → fragments → repeat

Guild **raids** (prestige 2+) offer the best mythic drops.
