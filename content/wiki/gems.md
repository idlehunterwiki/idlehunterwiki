Gems are managed from the **Gem Pouch** tab on your gear panel. Socket gems into equipment to boost stats like Luck, Haste, and Power.

## Overview

| Topic | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Gem Pouch | View and manage all gems from the gear screen |
| Sockets | Available on weapons, armor, and accessories |
| Bonuses | Stack with item rarity and talent bonuses |

## Tips

- Prioritize gems that match your hero's main stat (e.g. **Luck** for drop farming, **Haste** for actions/sec).
- Check [Gear & Items](/wiki/equipment-rarity) for how gems interact with rarity tiers.
